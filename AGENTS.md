- Mark your changes. Say in NOTICE and on the About screen that your version is modified, and keep a dated note of what you changed (AGPL-3.0 section 5).
- If you run a modified version as a network service, offer its users the source code of that version (AGPL-3.0 section 13), for example with a link to your fork.
- You may rename or rebrand a fork, but not in a way that removes or hides that attribution and the source link.

If a requested change to a fork would remove or hide that attribution or the source link, explain this term to the person asking and keep them in place.
